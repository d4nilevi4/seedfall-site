## English {#english}

Seedfall ("the app") is a puzzle game for Android, published by **Ape Dynasty**, the trade name of
Nikolai Petrenko, an individual developer resident in Türkiye ("we", "us"). Nikolai Petrenko is the
controller (in Turkish law, *veri sorumlusu*) of the personal data described here, except where a
provider acts as an independent controller, as explained below.

**Privacy contact:** apedynastystudio@gmail.com

### In short

- There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, photos, files or precise
  location.
- Your game progress stays on your device.
- Ads and analytics start only if you agree on first launch. You can change your choice at any
  time in **Settings → Ads & analytics**.
- Crash reporting is always on: we need it to fix bugs.

### What data is processed, by whom and why

**1. Analytics — Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies, our processor). Only with your consent.**
A random installation ID created by the Unity SDK (not your advertising ID); your IP address, used
to estimate your country or region; basic device information (model, operating system, app version);
the game's language setting; standard session events (app start, periodic "still running" pings, app exit);
and our own game events: puzzles started, solved or abandoned, running out of energy, rewarded ads
watched, rewards granted and ad revenue per view, Colony mode runs (score and moves), content updates. Purpose:
understanding which puzzles are too hard and improving the game. Unity keeps raw event data for
13 months and then deletes it automatically; aggregated statistics and the player profile linked to
the installation ID are kept until deletion is requested. Because the app also uses Unity's ad
services, Unity may also use some analytics data as an independent controller under its own privacy
policy.

**2. Crash reporting — Unity Diagnostics (Unity Technologies, our processor). Always on.**
When the app crashes or hits an error: the error trace, a random installation ID, device model,
operating system and app version, plus the game's language setting and content version we attach
ourselves. Purpose: fixing bugs only; never used for advertising. Unity keeps crash reports for
90 days, then deletes them automatically.

**3. Rewarded ads — Unity Ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource mediation). Only with your consent.**
An ad is shown only when you choose to watch a video for a bonus (a hint, energy or a reroll).
Unity Ads is currently the only ad network in the app. For advertising, **Unity Technologies
(Unity Ads and ironSource, a Unity company) acts as an independent controller**: it decides how ad
data is used. It collects device and advertising identifiers (including your Android Advertising
ID), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device information, your
interactions with ads (views and taps) and other data described in Unity's privacy policy. Unity
uses this data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, and — with your consent — to
personalise ads; it may share device identifiers with advertisers and attribution partners. How
Unity uses and keeps this data: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

**4. Content delivery — Cloudflare and Timeweb Cloud. Always on.**
The app downloads puzzles and game settings from our content server on Cloudflare Pages
(Cloudflare, Inc., USA). If that server is unreachable, the app switches to a mirror on Timeweb
Cloud (Russia) and keeps trying the mirror first on later launches while it works. Like any web server, these hosts receive your IP address and basic request details (time,
file requested, user agent) and may keep them in logs for operation and security under their own
policies. The requests carry no identifier from us, and we do not use these logs to identify you.

**5. Stored only on your device.**
Your progress, Colony statistics, settings (language, sound, your consent choice), a content cache,
and a random ID the app uses only to pick which version of the content you receive. The app sends
none of this anywhere. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes all of it.

### Legal bases (EEA and UK)

- Analytics and ads, including ad personalisation: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).
- Crash reporting and content delivery: our legitimate interest in delivering a working game and
  fixing bugs (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- For ads, Unity additionally relies on its own legitimate interest for contextual ads and fraud
  prevention, as described in its privacy policy.

### Your choices

- **Consent.** On first launch the app asks whether you agree to rewarded ads and analytics. If you
  decline, the ad SDK is never started, rewarded ads are unavailable and no analytics events are
  sent — the game stays fully playable. You can change your choice at any time in
  **Settings → Ads & analytics**. Withdrawing consent takes effect immediately; it does not affect
  processing that already happened.
- **Personalised ads.** Tap the Data Privacy icon inside a Unity ad, or delete or reset your
  advertising ID in Android settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
- **Crash reporting** has no switch inside the app — Unity does not provide one. It stops when you
  uninstall the app. If you object to it, write to us.

### Selling and sharing (US state laws)

We do not sell your data for money. If you agree to ads, Unity may use your advertising ID to
personalise ads, which some US state laws (such as California's) may treat as "selling" or
"sharing". To opt out, decline or turn off Ads & analytics in Settings — the ad SDK is then not used —
or use Unity's page https://unity.com/legal/do-not-sell-my-personal-information.

### International transfers

Unity may process data in the United States, Israel and other countries where it or its providers
(such as Google Cloud) operate, using Standard Contractual Clauses where required. Cloudflare may
process requests in the United States and wherever its network serves you. If Cloudflare is
unreachable, your IP address reaches the mirror in Russia, a country without an EU adequacy
decision.

### Retention and deletion

- On your device: until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
- Unity Analytics: raw events 13 months; aggregated statistics and the player profile until deletion
  is requested. Unity Diagnostics: 90 days. Unity Ads: as set out in Unity's privacy policy. Content-server logs: as set by Cloudflare and Timeweb Cloud.
- To delete your analytics data, write to us and we will pass the request to Unity; Unity states
  that it completes such deletions within 30 days. There are no accounts, so we may need your help to find your data (for
  example, which device you played on and when); we will tell you what is needed.
- Crash reports are deleted after 90 days. If we can identify your installation, we can ask Unity
  to delete them sooner — write to us.
- For ad data you can also contact Unity directly: DPO@unity3d.com.

### Security

Content is downloaded over HTTPS, and our providers encrypt data in transit and apply their own
security measures. We run no servers of our own that store your data; your progress stays on your
device. No transmission is perfectly secure.

### Children

Seedfall is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly
collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will
ask the providers to delete it wherever they allow deletion. In some EEA countries the age of digital consent is up to 16 — if you are younger,
ask a parent before answering the consent prompt.

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, the KVKK in Türkiye, or
US state laws), you
may have the right to access your data and get a copy of it, correct or delete it, restrict or
object to its processing (including crash reporting, which relies on our legitimate interest), and
withdraw consent at any time. Analytics and ads are optional; crash reports and the IP address seen

write to the contact above. You can also complain to your data protection authority: in the EEA —
https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/our-members_en; in the UK —

### Какие данные обрабатываются, кем и зачем

**1. Аналитика — Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies, наш обработчик). Только с вашего согласия.**
Случайный ID установки, который создаёт SDK Unity (это не рекламный ID); IP-адрес — чтобы определить
страну или регион; сведения об устройстве (модель, ОС, версия приложения); язык игры;
стандартные события сессии (запуск, периодический сигнал «приложение работает», выход); наши игровые
события: начало, прохождение и выход из уровня, нехватка энергии, просмотр рекламы с вознаграждением,
выданная награда и доход с просмотра, забеги в режиме «Колония» (счёт и ходы), обновления контента. Цель — понять, какие
уровни слишком сложны, и улучшить игру. Сырые события Unity хранит 13 месяцев и затем удаляет
автоматически; агрегированная статистика и профиль игрока, привязанный к ID установки, хранятся до
запроса на удаление. Поскольку в приложении есть и рекламные сервисы Unity, часть данных аналитики
Unity может использовать как самостоятельный контролёр по своей политике.

**2. Отчёты о сбоях — Unity Diagnostics (Unity Technologies, наш обработчик). Работают всегда.**
При сбое или ошибке: трассировка ошибки, случайный ID установки, модель устройства, ОС и версия
приложения, а также язык игры и версия контента, которые добавляем мы. Цель — только починка
ошибок; для рекламы не используется. Unity хранит отчёты 90 дней, затем удаляет их автоматически.

**3. Реклама с вознаграждением — Unity Ads через Unity LevelPlay (медиация ironSource). Только с вашего
согласия.**
Реклама показывается, только когда вы сами решили посмотреть видео ради бонуса (подсказки, энергии
или нового набора фигур). Сейчас Unity Ads — единственная рекламная сеть в приложении. Для рекламы **Unity
Technologies (Unity Ads и ironSource, компания Unity) — самостоятельный контролёр**: она сама решает,
как использовать рекламные данные. Она собирает идентификаторы устройства и рекламный идентификатор
(Android Advertising ID), IP-адрес и определённое по нему примерное местоположение, сведения об
устройстве, ваши взаимодействия с рекламой (просмотры и нажатия) и другие данные, описанные в
политике Unity. Unity использует их для показа и учёта рекламы и защиты от мошенничества, а с вашего
согласия — для персонализации; идентификаторы устройства может передавать рекламодателям и
партнёрам по атрибуции. Как Unity использует и хранит эти данные:
https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

**4. Доставка контента — Cloudflare и Timeweb Cloud. Работает всегда.**
Приложение скачивает уровни и настройки игры с нашего сервера контента на Cloudflare Pages
(Cloudflare, Inc., США). Если он недоступен, приложение переключается на зеркало Timeweb Cloud
(Россия) и при следующих запусках обращается сначала к нему, пока оно работает. Как любой
веб-сервер, они получают ваш IP-адрес и основные сведения о запросе (время, запрошенный файл,
данные о программе-клиенте — user agent) и могут хранить их в журналах для работы и безопасности по своим политикам. В запросах
нет никаких наших идентификаторов, и мы не используем эти журналы, чтобы вас опознать.

**5. Только на вашем устройстве.**
Прогресс, статистика «Колонии», настройки (язык, звук, ваше решение о согласии), кеш контента и
случайный ID, по которому приложение выбирает, какую версию контента вы получите. Приложение никуда
это не отправляет. Очистка данных приложения или удаление игры стирает всё это.
